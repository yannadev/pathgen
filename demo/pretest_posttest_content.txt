B. John has 12 apples and receives 5 more apples. How many does he have?

10. A classmate writes (-5) - (-9) = -14. Which rule correctly explains why this is incorrect and shows the correct solution?
A. Subtracting a negative is the same as adding a positive: (-5) - (-9) = (-5) + 9 = 4.
B. Subtracting a negative is the same as adding a negative: (-5) - (-9) = -5 - 9 = -14.
C. When subtracting, keep the first number and add the opposite of the second: (-5) - (-9) = -5 + 9 = 4.
D. Both A and C are correct explanations.
Answer: D
