ACTIVITY 1: OPERATIONS ON INTEGERS & RATIONAL NUMBERS

Covers: Lesson 1 (Integers) + Lesson 2 (Rational Numbers)

QUESTIONS:

1. What is the absolute value of -25?
A. -25
B. 0
C. 25
D. 50
Hint: Absolute value is the distance from zero. Distance is always positive.
Answer: C

2. What is the sum of 34 and (-56)?
A. -90
B. -22
C. 22
D. 90
Hint: Different signs — subtract 34 from 56. Answer takes the sign of the larger absolute value.
Answer: B

3. What is the result when (-68) is subtracted from 45?
A. -113
B. -23
C. 23
D. 113
Hint: 45 - (-68) = 45 + 68.
Answer: D

4. What is the quotient of (-288) divided by 16?
A. -18
B. -19
C. 18
D. 19
Hint: Negative ÷ positive = negative. 288 ÷ 16 = 18.
Answer: A

5. Evaluate: (-15) × 8
A. -120
B. -23
C. 120
D. 23
Hint: Negative × positive = negative. 15 × 8 = 120.
Answer: A

6. Evaluate: (-12) × (-11)
A. -132
B. -23
C. 23
D. 132
Hint: Negative × negative = positive. 12 × 11 = 132.
Answer: D

7. Solve: 26 - (-14) = ?
A. -40
B. -12
C. 12
D. 40
Hint: Subtracting a negative = adding its opposite: 26 + 14.
Answer: D

8. Solve: -18 - 7 = ?
A. -25
B. -11
C. 11
D. 25
Hint: -18 - 7 = -18 + (-7). Add negatives.
Answer: A

9. What is the product of (-8) × (-5) × 3?
A. -120
B. -40
C. 40
D. 120
Hint: (-8) × (-5) = 40. Then 40 × 3 = 120.
Answer: D

10. Simplify: 24 ÷ (-6) + 5
A. -9
B. -1
C. 1
D. 9
Hint: Division first: 24 ÷ (-6) = -4. Then -4 + 5 = 1.
Answer: C

11. Simplify: -7 × 5 + (-12) ÷ 4
A. -41
B. -38
C. 38
D. 41
Hint: -7 × 5 = -35. -12 ÷ 4 = -3. Add: -35 + (-3) = -38.
Answer: B

12. If a = -9 and b = -4, what is a - b?
A. -13
B. -5
C. 5
D. 13
Hint: -9 - (-4) = -9 + 4 = -5.
Answer: B

13. If x = -6 and y = -3, what is x × y + 10?
A. -28
B. -8
C. 8
D. 28
Hint: (-6) × (-3) = 18. 18 + 10 = 28.
Answer: D

14. Simplify: (-10 + 6) × (-4)
A. -64
B. -16
C. 16
D. 64
Hint: Parentheses first: -10 + 6 = -4. Then (-4) × (-4) = 16.
Answer: C

B. Yes, because 9 × 6 = 54 and you keep the negative sign
C. No, because multiplying two negative numbers gives a positive product
D. No, because the answer should be 45

16. What is 5/12 + 7/12 in simplest form?

B. 1/3

D. 1

17. Simplify: 7/10 - 3/5

Hint: LCD = 10. 3/5 = 6/10. 7/10 - 6/10 = 1/10.

21. Express 3/8 as a decimal.
